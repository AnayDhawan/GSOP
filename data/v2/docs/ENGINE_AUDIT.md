## Verdict

1. FAIL that matters: the v1 site statistic (best tile within 20 km, percentile among all gated tiles) is about 97 for a site with random features (97.5 to 99.2 measured, 97.1 analytic). Controls at 90.2 and 75.6, and Moon Haughton 85.8, sit at or below chance, so they say nothing about whether the method separates analogs from non-analogs.
2. The pre-registered control criterion (controls below 25) cannot be met by this statistic: the best of about 35 tiles landing at or below the 25th percentile has probability about 0.25^35. The same holds for any rerun with new columns, so a FAIL there carries no information about the new columns.
3. 25% of the Moon score weight (illumination) is dead for every Earth tile; the score ceiling is about 75. Ranks change little; the weights shown on the site overstate what the score uses.
4. Namib's match is real numbers, not NaN or zero fill: close roughness values plus very wide sigmas from the shackleton window spread (13.5 deg slope, 1292 m relief).
5. Mars positives below the 97 chance level (83.3, 61.3, 16.2) are the informative numbers; the percentile for the nearest single tile is not reported by v1. Not checked: Mars shuffle, the other profiles, the unit definitions of roughness across bodies.
